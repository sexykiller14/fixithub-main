Before turning ads on

  • Never click your own ads. That is a Google AdSense policy violation and will get the account banned.
  • Keep placements clear of navigation and buttons. Google requires ads to never sit where a visitor could mistake them for page UI.
  • If visitors are in the EU or UK, a certified consent-management message is required before AdSense loads. The site does not ship one, so adding AdSense without one does not satisfy those rules.
