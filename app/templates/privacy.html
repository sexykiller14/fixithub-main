Privacy

Plainly, because you should not have to guess what a site like this keeps about you.

The short version

  • •If you never create an account and never use the question box, we store nothing about you beyond what a web server logs.
  • •We do not run analytics, and we do not embed trackers from other companies.
  • •An account needs an email address. We do not send anything else to it.
  • •A question sent with the "Ask us anything" box is stored so it can be answered, whether or not you have an account. See below.
  • •You can delete your account and everything you wrote at any time, without asking us.

Questions you ask

The "Ask us anything" box does not ask who you are. When you send a question we store the words you wrote, the address of the page you sent it from, and a one-way hash of your IP address so repeated abuse is traceable. The hash cannot be turned back into an address.

If you were signed in when you sent it, we also record the email address on your account so an admin can tell your question apart from others. We do not attach an address you have not given us, and there is no field in the box that invites you to type one.

A question stays in the admin queue until it is answered or deleted. Answering it does not email you: the answer appears in the same box on this site, which is why the box keeps a random one-time code in your browser so it can fetch the answer later. Clearing your browser data discards that code, so an answer sent after you do so will not reach you. Nothing is used for advertising or profiling, and no question is ever published on a page.

What an account stores

Data held for a registered account
What Why
Email address To confirm the address is real before downloads are unlocked.
A one-way hash of your password To check a sign-in. We cannot reverse it, so nobody here can read your password, including us.
Your comments To show them on the page they were written for.
A hashed form of your IP address So repeated abuse is traceable. The hash cannot be turned back into an address.
When you last signed in Nothing more than that is recorded about your sessions.

What cookies are set

  • •A session cookie, so you stay signed in and so forms can be protected against cross-site request forgery. It contains no personal data.
  • •A theme cookie, so the site does not flash the wrong colours on every page.
  • •No advertising, profiling or third-party cookies. We do not embed anything from another company.

Comments

Comments are plain text and are read by a person before they appear. Do not post anything you would not want published, because an approved comment stays on the page.

Deleting your account

Sign in and use the delete option on your account page. That removes your email address, your password and all your comments immediately. We do not keep a copy, and we do not retain an anonymised version of what you wrote.

If you cannot sign in, contact the site owner and ask for the address to be removed.

Files you upload

If you send a minidump to the BSOD analyzer, it is parsed in memory and thrown away. Nothing is kept except a line in the admin log recording the filename, size and result, so abuse is visible. A dump is a snapshot of your memory, so we do not store it.

Children

This site is not aimed at children, and nobody under 13 should create an account here.

Changes

If this notice changes materially, the change will be visible in the site history rather than applied quietly.
