Before you open your PC

  • • Unplug everything and hold the power button for 10 seconds to drain residual power.
  • • Ground yourself by touching bare metal on the case before touching any component. Static can damage parts invisibly.
  • • Never open a power supply. The capacitors inside hold a lethal charge even when unplugged.
  • • Note which cable went where before disconnecting anything, and take photos as you go.
  • • If a fix involves opening the case and you are not confident, stop. A technician is cheaper than a damaged motherboard.
