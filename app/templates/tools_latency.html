Jitter matters more than the average

Calls and games suffer more from variation than from a consistently high number. A steady 90 ms is comfortable; a connection swinging between 10 ms and 300 ms is not, even though the average looks better. High jitter usually means Wi-Fi congestion, a congested uplink, or bufferbloat on a saturated connection.
