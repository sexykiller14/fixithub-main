Driver help

Every link below goes to an official vendor page. There are no third-party driver packs here, because those are how most driver problems start.

Download drivers from the manufacturer only

"Driver updater" software and sites that host every driver from every manufacturer install unsigned and mismatched drivers. Windows may refuse to load them, or they may cause crashes and instability. Use the links on this page, or the Microsoft Update Catalog, which is official and hosts signed drivers vendors have withdrawn.
